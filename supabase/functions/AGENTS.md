# Edge function rules

- Domestic market figures registered in the KOSIS series list (summarize-patent/kosis.ts) take their base value and CAGR from official statistics (refreshed every 30 days) and override AI-written numbers, so the same market always shows the same official figures.
- Market figures are pinned per market in market_reference (domestic KRW and global USD) with a trust status: official (KOSIS) and approved (admin-reviewed with a source) values are injected into the summary prompt as standards with their citation; new AI figures are stored only as ai_pending (no source, CAGR 0–30%), shown to the model as unverified, and every sentence they back is labelled "(AI 추정치)". All statuses are re-applied after generation as one set per market (base, CAGR, projections), and a trusted value is found by key, label or KOSIS series (pickTrustedRef) so a market never falls back to (AI 추정치) because of an old key; admins review ai_pending in 요약서 → 시장 표준값 검토.
- Accumulate only server-cache-verified, applicant-scoped original source excerpts through the shared analysis-case policy; never recycle AI claims, scores or market figures as truth, and audit policy/source/reference IDs without invalidating existing scores.
- Define summary section content budgets in the sectionBalance helper and version the prompt on policy changes; preserve full prose rather than truncating output.
- find-my-patents retrieves candidates only through search-patents and keeps scope/ranking rules in finderPolicy; AI rankings may only reference retrieved, applicant-scoped candidates so no patent is invented.
- filing-trends aggregates complete applicant-scoped KIPRIS pages, compares only fully disclosed years (18-month publication lag) and keeps AI summary sentences only when every number matches the computed statistics.
- Sentence-style searches filter and rank by title coverage of AI-extracted concept groups (core term + synonyms) in search-patents/nlRelevance.ts; abstract hits, conversational and generic words never qualify a result. isNaturalLanguageQuery here mirrors src/lib/queryIntent.ts — change both together.
- patent-chat answers only from data retrieved per question (search-patents results for the 6 institutes, the filing-trends snapshot, stored TRL values — no commercialization scores — and the fixed transfer guide), with routing/card rules in chatPolicy.ts; patent cards come from server data, never model text.
- filing-api `lib/` started as a copy of 에이프 `server/*.js` (process.env shim in lib/env.ts) and is now owned here: change it directly, never in kimjs3176-prog/koat-axlab.
